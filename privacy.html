Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

In short

PDF Accessibility Checker does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Your files never leave your Mac.

Your files

  • PDF files are analyzed locally, on your Mac, in a separate sandboxed helper process.
  • No file, file name, content or result is sent anywhere.
  • Fixed files are saved as new files next to the originals; the app does not keep copies.

Network

The app does not connect to the internet, with one exception: the in-app purchase of Pro (and restoring it) goes through Apple’s App Store. That transaction is handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; the developer receives no personal information about you.

No account, no analytics

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind in the app.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no trackers, no analytics and no external resources such as fonts or scripts from other servers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the pages, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact

For questions about privacy, write to Lagioni Apps Support at batesstamp.support@gmail.com.

PDF Accessibility Checker helps you check the accessibility of PDF files. It does not certify legal conformance with any law or standard.
